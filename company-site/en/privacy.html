What data we collect and why

What we collect

Project enquiry. The form on the Contact page validates your input in the browser and prepares a JSON file that you download or copy yourself. The form sends nothing to any server. Your details (name, email, company, optional phone, project description, budget) reach us only when you email us the enquiry.

Site chat. The chat is a simple program running entirely in your browser: it serves canned answers and never sends your questions anywhere off your device. We keep no chat history.

Tracking. The site uses no analytics, pixels, or cookies other than those strictly necessary to display it. We do not profile visitors.

Purpose and legal basis

We use enquiry data only to reply and to prepare discovery and a quote — steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Follow-up messages in the same thread rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): finishing the business conversation.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for 24 months after the last contact, then delete it — unless you ask for erasure sooner or a contract follows (then the data moves to project records under tax retention periods).

Who we share it with

Nobody except necessary technical operators: the site host and the mailbox operator (names added once providers are picked). No transfers outside the EEA are planned — confirmed per operator once picked. We sell no data to anyone.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. To exercise them, write to the contact address above. You also have the right to complain to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Providing data is voluntary, but without an email address we cannot reply to an enquiry. Last updated: November 2026.
