Services

Four services, one way of working

Every engagement starts with paid discovery and a written result. Every build has a fixed price, a written scope with exclusions, and an acceptance test. Every build ends with a care-plan proposal.

  1. B

    One-workflow automation — hero offer

    One repetitive workflow end-to-end: forms → spreadsheet → email → report; scheduled data transfer; invoice/document intake and extraction; report generation. Named systems, documented handoff, 30-day fix window in the price.

    For ops managers and founders drowning in spreadsheets and copy-paste. We start with the most painful workflow — we never pitch a company-wide transformation.

  2. C

    Connecting systems

    Two existing systems that don't talk to each other: CRM with webshop, bookings with calendar, ERP with reporting. A small internal panel on top — only when needed. Fixed price per connection, runbook included.

    For companies with 2–3 SaaS tools and manual bridges between them. Usually found in discovery, never pitched blind.

  3. A

    Websites with aftercare

    A small enquiry-winning site (profile, form, booking), responsive, with basic SEO and analytics. Then a monthly care plan: updates, backups, uptime checks, small content changes.

    For companies with an outdated template site and no in-house developer. No webshops.

  4. D

    Private AI — add-on only

    A module inside the above, where sensitive data demands it: local or EU-hosted document extraction, classification, and drafting, with visible routing and usage reports. Never standalone "AI agents", never autonomous bots without human approval gates.

    For legal, HR, finance, and medical admin that would otherwise refuse cloud AI.

Honest limits

What we don't do

  • Mobile apps and ecommerce platforms.
  • Branding/design agency work.
  • 24/7 SLAs.
  • Autonomous consumer bots without human supervision.
  • Regulated cores (medical, finance).
  • Payments beyond links, multi-tenant SaaS, private-AI modules — outside a paid discovery that settles them.
Why this list is here. Every "no" above saves both sides a wasted month. If your need lands on it, we say so in the first reply — not after three meetings.

Terms in brief

What the agreement looks like

  • One-page quote/SOW per engagement: scope, exclusions, client inputs, timeline, acceptance criteria, price, payment schedule.
  • Payment: 30–50% upfront, balance on acceptance; care plans monthly in advance.
  • Acceptance: named criteria tested within 7–14 days of delivery; then a 30-day fix window for defects against spec.
  • IP: finished deliverables belong to the client on final payment; reusable libraries and templates stay with us.
  • Confidentiality: every quote and SOW is confidential by default — your scope and price never appear in other quotes or case studies without your written consent.
  • Data/GDPR: controller/processor roles, credentials via password-manager share with rotation, backups and offboarding — settled before touching personal data.

Full master terms need a lawyer's and accountant's review before first use — we'll tell you where they stand.
