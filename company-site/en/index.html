Why us

A small vendor for small jobs — on purpose

  • Jobs below a software house's threshold. A one-workflow automation costs less than their quote process. Our niche is their floor.
  • Senior-only, no handoff. You talk to the person who builds. No account-manager → analyst → junior telephone game.
  • Local on-site (Skoczów radius). Twenty minutes away beats a travel fee or a video call from Warsaw.
  • Narrow promise plus a paid exit ramp. We sell one outcome, and discovery may conclude with an honest "don't do this".
  • Speed. No sprint zero, no procurement theater: discovery to working workflow in weeks.
An honest "no" is part of the offer. Multi-team builds, 24/7 SLAs, enterprise procurement, regulated cores — not us. We'll say so before you spend a euro.

How we work

Three steps, each with a written result

  1. Paid discovery. On-site or remote audit: 1–2 weeks, a report with a recommendation — or a "don't do this". The report stays with you.
  2. One automation at a fixed price. Written scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, and a 30-day fix window in the price.
  3. Aftercare. A care plan attached to every build: updates, monitoring, small changes.
First clients: founding-client terms. Full price and extended care — in exchange for a testimonial, a logo, and case-study rights. A trade, not a beg.

FAQ

Questions we hear before the first call

Why fixed price instead of hourly?
You buy an outcome, not a CV. "This workflow, working, for this much, by this date" needs no reputation to evaluate. Hourly headlines are for people with reputations.
What if automation doesn't pay off?
The discovery report tells you — with payback math computed together. A "no" is a delivered outcome, not a failure.
What does it cost?
Every build is an individual fixed quote after discovery; we publish no price list. Roughly, matching the contact form: discovery and light fixes — under €2k; full workflows — usually €2–5k; larger integrations — €5–15k. Above that scale we tell you plainly whether it is for us.
Who builds it?
The founder, a senior, personally — the same person you talk to. We don't invent a team or office photos.
Do you work outside the area?
Yes — remotely across Poland and the EU. On-site within about an hour's drive of Skoczów.
