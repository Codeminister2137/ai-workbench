# Inbox decision brief template

Copy the body below when filing `decision` items. Keep every section and the
blank lines between them; one unbroken paragraph is unreadable. Mirrors the
AGENTS.md "How to ask" brief format.

```text
Decision needed: [FILL: one sentence — what must be chosen]

Why it matters: [FILL: concrete effects on behavior, users, compatibility, privacy, data, or future constraints]

Options:
[FILL: each viable materially different choice, one per line, lettered]

Pros and cons:
[FILL: material benefits and costs/risks for EVERY listed option — no one-sided comparisons]

Technical recommendation: [FILL: the technically preferable option and why it fits the known goals and constraints — or what uncertainty prevents a recommendation]

Question: [FILL: the exact question requiring the owner's choice]
```

Also set `options` to the short choice labels (first = recommended, suffixed
` (Recommended)`), and keep the full trade-offs in the body.
