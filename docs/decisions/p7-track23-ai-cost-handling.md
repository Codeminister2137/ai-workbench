# P7 Track 2.3: delivery costs and client AI consumption

## Decision and owner rationale

For client software built through our code factory, our AI use is our operating
cost. The client pays the agreed scoped delivery fee; our internal AI consumption
is not a separate client charge or pass-through allowance. We choose how to
deliver and what to offer and contract for. Cost attribution is advisory for
estimating operating costs and margins: it may inform the price and may be
disclosed when useful to a client, but is not a client invoice or entitlement.
The stored source note explicitly says we should not charge clients for our AI
use, resolving the missing negation in the closeout prompt's copy.

AI consumption in a product sold to a client is separate and depends on the
client's decision. C (client-owned account and direct provider billing) is the
strongest preferred option there: the client buys access to our provider-agnostic,
expandable product and obtains/manages API keys and credits. We may help with
onboarding while maintaining and expanding the product; assistance does not
transfer all account responsibilities to us.

Extracting existing capabilities into a separately maintained product is a
conditional commercial opportunity if discovery demonstrates sufficient demand.
It is not immediate development authorization, a launch commitment or a claim
of proven market uniqueness.

## Alternatives and consequences

- A, actual-cost pass-through: flexible attribution, but variable client bills
  and reconciliation; not selected for our delivery AI.
- B, capped allowance with overage pause: bounds a separately agreed budget,
  but adds admission/pause and reconciliation duties. The earlier recommendation
  is superseded for delivery AI; no such runtime controls are required here.
- C, client-owned direct billing: client control and no founder financing of
  consumption, but onboarding and variable provider bills remain. Preferred for
  a future AI-enabled product, subject to client choice.
- D, fixed scoped bundle: predictable delivery fee and efficiency incentives,
  with operating-cost risk borne by us. Selected for software delivery; estimate
  costs and contingency internally and agree scope changes separately.

The owner's distinction corrects the original brief's conflation of development
AI with client production consumption. No numeric prices, unlimited production
usage, spending grants, credentials/data-routing permissions, account changes,
automated metering, hard caps or billing implementation follow from this choice.
Reviewed client-specific scope, pricing, legal/accounting and data terms remain
required before a quote, public promise or charge. Other P7 tracks stay separate.
