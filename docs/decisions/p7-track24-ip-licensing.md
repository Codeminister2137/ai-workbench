## Legal drafting gate

[Polish Copyright Act, consolidated 2025 publication](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2025/24/text/I/D20250024.pdf)
was inspected as background, particularly Articles 41, 53, 67–68 and 74–77.
Nonexclusivity permits licensing others; it does not itself settle source
delivery, modification, maintainer permissions or sublicensing. Counsel must
verify law at signing and draft exploitation fields, modification permissions,
geography/duration, payment/interim rights, termination/continuity,
transferability and third-party/background schedules. Do not equate perpetual
with guaranteed irrevocability. Assignment/exclusive licensing have formal
requirements; source delivery differs from copyright ownership. No legal
wording or tax consequence is approved here.
